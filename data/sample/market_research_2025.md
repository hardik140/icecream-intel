# Frosty Scoops — Market Research Brief 2025

## Market Overview
The Indian packaged ice cream market grew about 12% in 2025. Growth is strongest in tier-1 cities and in premium tubs priced above ₹200. Summer (April to June) accounts for roughly 40% of annual volume.

## Competitors

### Polar Delight
National leader with about 28% share. Competes mainly on price; its 500 ml Vanilla sells at ₹160 versus our ₹189. In 2025 it launched a real-fruit mango range in North India that advertises "100% Alphonso pulp", which directly targets our Mango Alphonso customers.

### Kulfi Kraft
Regional player strong in the North and West, focused on traditional flavours such as Kesar Pista and Malai. Prices are similar to ours.

### Snowberry Gelato
Premium brand priced at ₹300+ per tub, available mainly on quick-commerce apps in metros. Gaining share among young urban buyers.

## Consumer Trends

### Health and Sugar-Free
Searches for "sugar-free ice cream" rose 70% year on year. Diabetic and fitness-focused buyers are willing to pay a 20–30% premium. Few competitors have a sugar-free range that tastes good, which is an opportunity.

### Quick-Commerce
Quick-commerce apps (10-minute delivery) became the fastest-growing channel for ice cream in metros, reaching about one third of urban tub sales by late 2025. Delivery temperature control is the main quality risk in this channel.

### Real Ingredients
Consumers increasingly check labels and reward "real fruit" and "no artificial colour" claims. Artificial taste is the top reason for switching brands in our survey.

## Pricing
Our average price is 8–15% above Polar Delight and 30% below Snowberry. Price increases of 5% in January 2025 did not reduce volume for core flavours.

## Opportunities
1. Sugar-free range extension (chocolate, butterscotch).
2. Quick-commerce-specific packaging and smaller pack sizes.
3. Restoring the "real Alphonso" positioning to defend against Polar Delight's mango launch.
