# Frosty Scoops — Customer Review Report 2025

## Summary
This report summarises 4,812 customer reviews collected from January to September 2025 across quick-commerce apps, supermarket feedback cards and our parlour QR survey. Average rating fell from 4.3 in 2024 to 4.1 in 2025. Most of the drop comes from two flavours: Mango Alphonso and Chocolate.

## Ratings by Flavour

### Mango Alphonso
Average rating 3.4 (down from 4.5 in 2024). Complaints started in February 2025 and are concentrated in the North region (Delhi NCR, Chandigarh, Lucknow). The most common phrases were "tastes artificial", "not like real mango anymore" and "too sweet". 61% of negative Mango reviews mention taste; 22% mention colour being "too bright orange". Several long-time customers wrote that they switched to a competitor's mango flavour. Reviews from the South and West are mostly unchanged, which matches the new pulp being rolled out first to the North plant.

### Chocolate
Average rating 3.9. Taste is praised, but 38% of negative reviews complain that the tub arrives partly melted, almost all from quick-commerce orders in May and June. Customers blame long delivery times and thin packaging. Parlour and supermarket buyers rarely mention melting.

### Sugar-Free Vanilla
Average rating 4.6, our highest. Customers say it is "the only sugar-free ice cream that tastes like real ice cream". Frequent requests: a sugar-free chocolate and a smaller 250 ml pack.

### Cookie Crunch
Average rating 4.4 since its July 2024 launch. Praised for big cookie pieces; some reviews say the price (₹262) is high compared with Vanilla.

### Other Flavours
Vanilla (4.3), Butterscotch (4.2), Strawberry (4.0) and Kesar Pista (4.4) are stable with no major complaint themes. A small number of Strawberry reviews call it "icy".

## Packaging and Delivery
Packaging complaints doubled in summer 2025, driven by quick-commerce orders. Reviews mention lids opening, tubs softening and no insulation. Supermarket customers raise packaging issues far less often.

## Recommendations from the Review Team
1. Investigate the Mango Alphonso recipe and pulp supplier change urgently; consider reverting in the North.
2. Introduce insulated pouches for quick-commerce deliveries during April–June.
3. Prioritise a sugar-free chocolate launch given strong Sugar-Free Vanilla demand.
4. Test a 250 ml pack size for premium flavours such as Cookie Crunch and Kesar Pista.
