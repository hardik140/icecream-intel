# Frosty Scoops — Operations and Supplier SOP

## Cold Chain and Storage

### Storage Temperature
All finished ice cream must be stored at −18 °C or colder. Distribution trucks must hold −20 °C. Any batch exposed above −12 °C for more than 30 minutes must be quarantined and checked by QA.

### Shelf Life
Unopened tubs have a shelf life of 12 months from manufacture when stored at −18 °C. Retailers must follow first-expiry-first-out stock rotation.

### Quick-Commerce Handover
Dark stores must keep ice cream in dedicated freezers. Delivery partners must use insulated bags; maximum time outside the freezer is 15 minutes. Insulated pouches for summer deliveries are under evaluation (pilot planned for April 2026).

## Hygiene and Quality Checks
Every batch is tested for microbial count, fat percentage and overrun (target 60–70% for premium tubs). Production lines are cleaned after every flavour changeover. QA signs off each batch before dispatch.

## Suppliers

### Milk and Cream
Supplied by Green Valley Dairy Co-op under a 3-year contract (2024–2026) with monthly price revision linked to the milk procurement index.

### Mango Pulp
Until December 2024 mango pulp came from Ratnagiri Orchards (100% Alphonso pulp, ₹180/kg). From January 2025 the North plant switched to SunPulp Foods (blended Totapuri and Alphonso pulp with added colour, ₹125/kg) to cut costs by about 30%. The South and West plants were scheduled to switch in July 2025, but the rollout was paused after the first quality complaints. The contract with SunPulp Foods can be ended with 60 days' notice.

### Cocoa
Imported cocoa from Coastal Cocoa Traders. Prices rose 18% in 2025 because of global cocoa shortages.

### Packaging
Tubs and lids are supplied by PackRight Industries. The current lid design is not tamper-evident, and the thin-wall tub introduced in 2024 saves 12% on material cost.

## Recipe Change Policy
Any recipe or ingredient-supplier change must pass a blind taste test with at least 50 consumers and a sign-off from the Head of Product. Note: the January 2025 mango pulp change was approved on cost grounds before the consumer taste test was completed.
